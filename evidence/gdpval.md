**Vendor capture.** OpenAI authored, selected, and still owns the full task corpus (1,320 tasks; only 220 "gold" public) (https://openai.com/index/gdpval/, https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04374v1). AA runs an independent harness (Stirrup) and leaderboard, but its 3-judge panel is GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.8 (https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking): OpenAI's own model partly grades itself and rivals on OpenAI-written tasks. AA's disclosed funding is a thin $250K seed (AI Grant, Build Club) (https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/680302-90); no lab payment confirmed, but disclosure is opaque. No proven-access scandal found for GDPval itself, but 1,100 non-gold tasks + references stay privately held by the creator, unaudited: the same structural shape as OpenAI's disclosed FrontierMath pre-access (https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/19/ai-benchmarking-organization-criticized-for-waiting-to-disclose-funding-from-openai/).

**Contamination/methodology.** OpenAI's own automated grader agrees with human experts only 65.7% of the time; human-human agreement is itself just 70.8% (https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04374v1). Epoch AI's independent review found over half the tasks need live web access and ~5% assume now-outdated viewpoints: real hindsight/leak exposure, no contamination controls documented (https://epoch.ai/blog/what-do-economic-value-benchmarks-tell-us). Shell+web agentic harness, no published gaming red-team.

**Saturation.** Epoch called GDPval the benchmark "closest to being saturated" of those it reviewed (GPT-5.2 Pro ~74% win+tie) on OpenAI's original win-rate scale (same source). AA's v2 Elo rebaseline restored headroom, but current snapshots show the #1/#2 gap near AA's own ~1% CI floor: noise at the very top, real spread further down the pack (https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa).

**Gaming history.** None found specific to GDPval/GDPval-AA: unlike LM Arena's Llama-4-Maverick swap (https://www.theregister.com/2025/04/08/meta_llama4_cheating/) or FrontierMath's disclosed OpenAI pre-access above. Genuine absence, but only ~10 months of adversarial scrutiny.

**Staleness.** Leaderboard reflects mid-2026 model releases and is versioned to v2: actively maintained, though no public "last-updated" stamp (https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa).

**Case for.** Real deliverables from ~14-year-experience professionals across 44 occupations, independently re-scored by a cross-vendor blind judge panel, widely mirrored by third-party leaderboards. Best used as a directional signal of professional-deliverable quality: discount any near-top gap under ~50 Elo as noise, and treat the OpenAI-owned task corpus as an unaudited structural conflict, not a proven one.

VERDICT: caveat | GDPval / GDPval-AA v2 | OpenAI owns/authors the task corpus and self-judges via GPT-5.5; ~30% grading noise; top-2 gap is statistical noise.
