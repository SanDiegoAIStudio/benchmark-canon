**Methodology integrity.** The live page (openrouter.ai/rankings, fetched 2026-07-17) still reads "Live LLM rankings based on benchmarks and real data", but the ranking mechanic is token-volume telemetry, not benchmark scoring (news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44814793; enterprisedna.co/directories/models/openrouter-leaderboard). It's OpenRouter self-reporting its own pipes with no independent audit found. Separately, OpenRouter has publicly acknowledged "silent quantization", different providers serve lower-precision variants under the same model name, different enough that OpenRouter shipped a paid "Exacto" tier to guarantee full precision (openrouter.ai/blog/insights/model-routing; github.com/RooCodeInc/Roo-Code/issues/11325), so quality behind one ranked "model" varies invisibly.

**Saturation/discrimination.** Fails at the frontier. Price and free-tier promos dominate over capability.

**Strongest case FOR:** as raw macro adoption/spend telemetry, what's actually shipped into production, Chinese-model share trends, cost-driven migration, it's real and hard to fake at the multi-week aggregate level.
