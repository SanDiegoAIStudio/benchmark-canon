**1. Vendor capture/funding**: Maintained by NTU S-Lab + Shanghai AI Lab + CUHK/Sun Yat-sen/Nanjing academics (github.com/Vchitect/VBench, vchitect.github.io/VBench-project). Funding is public/academic: Singapore MOE AcRF Tier-2 + NTU NAP, China NSFC, Shanghai STCSM, Shanghai AI Lab itself (arxiv.org/abs/2411.13503): no disclosed vendor payment. But Shanghai AI Lab is state-backed with an explicit mandate to advance the Chinese AI industry (merics.org/en/comment/profile-shanghai-ai-lab-driving-both-ai-safety-and-development), and Chinese vendors (Alibaba/Wan, Kuaishou/Kling, ByteDance/Seedance) dominate current entries: a structural conflict, not proven payment. Submissions are vendor-generated videos via Google Form (github.com/Vchitect/VBench): cherry-picking happens upstream of the automated score.

**2. Methodology**: VBench-2.0 scores via VLM/LLM judges + anomaly detectors, calibrated to human preference (arxiv.org/abs/2503.21755): standard LLM-judge bias surface, notably judging physics/causality with models known to be weak at physics/causality. Documented reproducibility gap: independently reproduced scores run below vendor-published ones (CFG-scale/prompt-extension sensitivity). An open, unanswered GitHub issue (github.com/Vchitect/VBench/issues/202, filed 2026-04-22) asks how to reproduce Wan2.2's claimed 84.7%: still unresolved.

**3. Saturation**: Original 16-dim VBench looks saturated at the open-source top: Wan 2.1→2.2 moved only 83.1%→84.7% across a full model generation (awesomeagents.ai/leaderboards/video-generation-benchmarks-leaderboard). VBench-2.0 still discriminates, Veo3 66.7%, Vidu Q1 62.7%, LongCat-Video 62.1% total; physics/commonsense sub-scores cluster 50-70% (arxiv.org/pdf/2510.22200), real spread, not saturated yet.

**4. Gaming history**: No documented cheating scandal or public exposure surfaced in this search: absence of evidence, not proof of clean.

**5. Liveness**: HF leaderboard Space (huggingface.co/spaces/Vchitect/VBench_Leaderboard) last modified 2026-04-01, ~3.5 months stale as of today. No verified newest-gen entries (Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Wan 2.5) found on the canonical board; most circulating VBench-2.0 numbers trace to individual vendor tech-report tables (e.g., LongCat-Video's own report) running the open eval code, not one continuously-curated ranking.

**FOR**: Peer-reviewed (CVPR'24 highlight), transparent open-source methodology, real human-calibration work, no vendor-payment found, and VBench-2.0's physics/causality axis is a genuine, currently-discriminating research signal. Trustworthy as directional capability-gap research, not as a live "who's #1 today" ranking.
