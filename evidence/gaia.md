**Origin (lens 1, capture):** Legitimate non-commercial academic construct: Meta-FAIR/Meta-GenAI + HuggingFace + AutoGPT researchers (Mialon, Fourrier, Wolf, LeCun, Scialom), arXiv 2311.12983, reviewed on OpenReview (openreview.net/forum?id=fibxvahvs3). No pay-to-play or paid-placement evidence found: low capture risk, a rare point in its favor.

**Contamination/methodology (lens 2):** The 165-question validation split is openly public with answers on the HF dataset repo (huggingface.co/datasets/gaia-benchmark/GAIA: "fully public dev set"). UC Berkeley RDI (Apr 2026) scored ~98% by loading those public answers at runtime plus exploiting the official grader's over-permissive `normalize_str` (strips whitespace/punctuation so garbled strings still match), deliberately missing one question per level to dodge the anti-cheat filter (rdi.berkeley.edu/blog/trustworthy-benchmarks-cont). Separately, "Scaffold Effects on GAIA" (arXiv 2606.08529, Jun 2026) shows agent-harness choice alone swings scores on identical models, concluding leaderboard rankings aren't comparable capability signals.

**Saturation (lens 3):** Levels 1-2 reported near-ceiling/solved by frontier systems; Level 3 still resists: partial discrimination survives only in the hardest third of the set.

**Gaming history (lens 4):** The RDI exploit above is dated, specific, reproducible, and institution-credible: same automated benchmark-breaking research class flagged in the brief, independently confirming it against GAIA specifically.

**Staleness/liveness (lens 5):** Princeton HAL, the most rigorous third-party host, explicitly paused new-model GAIA updates to pivot to a "reliability" framework (hal.cs.princeton.edu/gaia); its own top entry (Claude Sonnet 4.5, 74.55%, Sept 2025) is ~10 months stale and was scored on the leaked validation split, not the private test set. Meta+HF themselves shipped GAIA2 as an explicit successor for dynamic, read/write agent environments (huggingface.co/datasets/meta-agents-research-environments/gaia2), tacitly retiring v1. Circulating public numbers range 44.8%-92%+ across trackers (official HF leaderboard vs HAL vs third-party snapshots) with no reconciled canonical source, and none independently sandboxed on the private test set.

**FOR case:** Peer-reviewed, non-commercial origin; genre-defining task design (reasoning+browsing+tools+multimodality); Level 3 tasks still separate frontier systems when scored honestly under sandboxed test-set conditions. Trustworthy only as a historical/directional signal of general-assistant tool-use progress through ~2025, never as a live cited leaderboard number.
