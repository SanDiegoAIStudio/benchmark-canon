**Vendor capture (severe).** Sierra AI owns tau2-bench outright (github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench, MIT license). Sierra was cofounded and is run by Bret Taylor, simultaneously OpenAI's board chairman: confirmed by CNBC in 2025 and again 2026 (cnbc.com/2026/05/04/bret-taylor-sierra-fundraise-openai.html; cnbc.com/2025/09/04). OpenAI models are scored on the leaderboard Taylor's company operates. Taylor recuses from OpenAI-board matters touching Sierra (cheekypint.substack.com/p/bret-taylor-of-sierra-on-ai-agents): that manages the OpenAI-side conflict, not the reverse: his company grading a board he chairs. Sierra's May 2026 $950M round was co-led by Google's GV (techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious), and Gemini is also scored. Two major labs on the leaderboard are financially/governance-entangled with the scorer.

**Methodology.** Grading combines programmatic state/action checks with an LLM user-simulator (pinned to GPT-4o); runs that vary the simulator aren't comparable to official numbers (behaviorlayer.ai/evaluate/tau-bench). Princeton's Holistic Agent Leaderboard independently found Sierra's official "Few Shot" baseline had literal test-set examples leaked into its demonstration file, contaminating ~$1,000 of runs before discovery and getting pulled (arxiv.org/pdf/2510.11977). That's the predecessor repo, not confirmed on tau2-bench itself, but same team/lineage.

**Saturation:** not saturated at the frontier. Sierra's own newest banking_knowledge domain shows top models (GPT-5.2 high-reasoning) around 25% success (sierra.ai/blog/bench-advancing-agent-benchmarking-to-knowledge-and-voice), and pass^k curves show even 60%-pass^1 agents collapsing toward 25% by k=8 (hippocampus-garden.com/pass_k). Real headroom exists.

**Staleness:** v1.0.1 (July 2026) changed banking_knowledge grading; pre/post scores explicitly non-comparable (github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench). Circulating third-party numbers are a mess: aggregators disagree wildly (24-of-440, 226, 35, "38" models cited across pricepertoken.com, llm-stats.com, artificialanalysis.ai) and one aggregator (benchlm.ai/benchmarks/tauBench) refuses to publish taubench.com's own raw numbers, calling them undocumented. I could not load taubench.com directly (403).

**Case for it:** pass^k is a genuinely rare reliability metric most agent benchmarks skip; the harness is open/MIT and externally auditable (how HAL caught the leak); Sierra publishes self-damaging numbers (25%) and responded to outside critique (SABER-driven task fixes). Trustworthy only as a relative pass^k reliability signal read from the primary source and pinned to an exact version tag, not as a neutral cross-vendor ranking, and never via third-party aggregator numbers.
